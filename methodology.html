Methodology

Our research framework

Each report examines a stock from several disciplines, then weighs the evidence separately for long-term investors, swing traders, day traders and options traders.

The process

1 · DataMarket prices, filings and macro data
2 · AnalysisFundamental, forensic, macro, technical and flow work
3 · PlansTrading and options ideas built from that analysis
4 · RiskEvery plan checked against strict risk rules
5 · MemoA verdict for each time horizon

Disciplines

▤

Fundamentals & valuation

Business quality, return on invested capital versus cost of capital, multiples, DCF and reverse DCF, and a bear / base / bull fair-value range.

⌕

Accounting quality & events

Earnings-quality screens (Beneish M-Score, Altman Z, Piotroski F), SEC filing red flags, dilution, insider filings and the event calendar.

◍

Macro, policy & credit

Market regime, interest rates and the yield curve, volatility, credit spreads, sector leadership and the stock's macro sensitivities.

⟋

Technical analysis

Trend stage, trend template, momentum, volatility, volume, relative strength and key support / resistance levels.

⇄

Positioning & sentiment

Short interest, insider and institutional activity, options positioning and news tone.

⚡

Trading plans

Day-trade, swing and trend-following plans with defined entries, stops and targets.

◇

Options

Defined-risk option structures priced from the live options chain.

⛨

Risk management

Position sizing and risk rules applied to every plan: stops, reward-to-risk, maximum risk and no undefined-risk options.

Principles

Evidence first

Indicators, valuations, forensic scores and option prices are calculated directly from the data. Each conclusion cites the evidence behind it, and missing data is reported as unknown.

Risk before return

Plans with stops on the wrong side, poor reward-to-risk, excessive risk or undefined-risk options are rejected.

Horizon matters

A stock can be attractive for a long-term investor and unattractive for a swing trader at the same time. Each horizon gets its own verdict.

Known limitations

Free data can be delayed or incomplete. DCF models are mechanical starting points. Reports are snapshots and are not updated after publication.
